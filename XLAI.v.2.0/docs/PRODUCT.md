# Product

XLAI is a Communication Intelligence Platform focused on coaching users before they send messages.

The product exists to help people pause, understand tone and intent, reduce conflict, and rewrite messages without stripping away authenticity. The user stays in control at every step: XLAI coaches, analyzes, and suggests, but it does not decide what the user sends.

## Current scope

- Chat-based communication coaching
- AI-assisted rewrite and refine flow
- Tone and communication risk analysis
- Coach modes for different levels of guidance
- Journal for reflection
- Insights for behavior and pattern review
- Privacy-first message retention

## What this is not

- Not therapy
- Not medical advice
- Not legal advice
- Not a crisis service

## Product goals

- Coach before generating
- Teach, do not replace human communication
- Reduce conflict without removing authenticity
- Keep privacy first by default
- Avoid model training on user data unless the user clearly opts in

## Current status

XLAI is a strong beta with a working messaging core, coach/analyzer flows, journal and insights surfaces, and backend persistence. The remaining work is primarily production hardening, privacy completion, safety handling, testing, and identity/personalization cleanup.
